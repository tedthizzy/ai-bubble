1. **High - The valuation and financing leg is absent from the main bubble conclusion.** [OpenAI's March 31 funding release](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/) says it closed a round with $122 billion of *committed capital* at an $852 billion post-money valuation, with Amazon, NVIDIA and SoftBank anchoring it and Microsoft participating. It also says it was generating $2 billion per month then. [Anthropic's May 28 Series H announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h) says $65 billion raised at a $965 billion post-money valuation, including $15 billion of previously committed hyperscaler investments, while its revenue run rate had crossed $47 billion earlier that month. These are direct evidence of equity valuations and supplier-investor links in the financing chain. The current report gives the revenue paces but omits these financing marks. Add them near the answer-first conclusion and keep the distinctions: committed round capital is not cash receipts from customers; a post-money equity value divided by an annualized monthly pace is not an audited trailing revenue multiple; the two company paces are different dates and definitions. The omission limits a conclusion about valuation running ahead of cash flow, which is part of this repo's bubble question.

2. **High - The video's model-lab GW denominators need a direct disposition.** The report rejects the video's 15-to-45/55 GW global stock estimate and $50 billion per GW revenue conversion, but it does not explicitly address the transcript's premises that OpenAI and Anthropic already have about 5 GW each, will share 30 GW of new capacity, and that Anthropic's SpaceX contract is for 300 MW. [Anthropic's April AWS announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-amazon-compute) calls 5 GW an *up-to* capacity commitment over ten years and says nearly 1 GW of Trainium2/3 should be online by end-2026. Its [Google/Broadcom announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/google-broadcom-partnership-compute) says next-generation TPU capacity starts coming online in 2027. Neither establishes Anthropic operating 5 GW now. The prior [local SpaceX source memo](spacex_adjacency.md) attributes about 325,000 GPUs and about 1 GW across the two whole clusters to the [prospectus cited by the report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026042639/spaceexplorationtechnologi.htm); that memo does not establish 300 MW dedicated to Anthropic. The SEC prospectus was too large for this independent web fetch and was not yet in the local current acquisition cache, so verify its exact wording again when that document lands. Until then, identify the 300 MW denominator as unverified and avoid treating contractual GW as present utilization.

# BDC AI/DC exposure cards — the S3′ set membership evidence

**Carded 2026-06-12** for amendment A1 ([preregistered_signals.md](preregistered_signals.md)): the original S3 treated "top-4 listed BDC" discounts as an AI-funding-chain proxy and fired confirming on registration day via FSK −41%. Carding each book shows that was an **attribution error** — and the corrected signal reads *neutral*, not confirming. Sources are press, transcripts, and company IR (press_reported tier; schedule-of-investments exhibit verification pending EDGAR access).

## The headline finding

**Direct neocloud/GPU lending sits in private vehicles (Blackstone, Blue Owl, Apollo, PIMCO, BlackRock private funds), not in the listed BDCs.** The listed-BDC discount series was never a clean proxy for the AI funding chain. What the listed books carry is mostly *AI-disruption mark risk on conventional software loans* — a different exposure entirely. This weakens S3 as a signal (documented in the amendment) and strengthens the [limits-to-arbitrage](limits_to_arbitrage.md) finding: even the funding chain's listed windows don't cleanly hold the asset.

## Known residual confounds (carded, not hidden)

1. All eight took Q1-2026 NAV marks from the same credit-spread-widening event — itself partly AI-sentiment-driven — so exposed-vs-control moves are not fully independent of the factor being isolated.
2. Exposure shares are press/transcript-derived; schedule-of-investments exhibit verification upgrades this card when EDGAR access is restored.
3. BXSL's "exposure" is one named commitment plus a platform halo; if Firmus is small in FV terms, BXSL's differential is mostly software-mark beta. Exhibit verification will size it.

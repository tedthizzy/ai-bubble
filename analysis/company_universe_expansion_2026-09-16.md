# Company universe expansion, evidence through September 16, 2026

This extends the [22-CIK filing panel](case_zero_financials_2026-09-16_v4.md), the [existing company register](company_tracking_2026-09-16.md), and the [semiconductor supply review](semiconductor_supply_2026-09-16.md). It tracks an entity when a dated primary source supplies an economically relevant observation. A disclosed annualized run rate, recognized GAAP revenue, signed lease, financed equipment, rack delivered to a customer, and prospective campus are separate observations. Company releases are evidence of what the company disclosed; they do not independently verify customer utilization or future delivery. Private-company cash flow, debt outstanding, and capex are unknown unless a specific source below states them.

## Newly public and material financing counterparties

- **Cerebras Systems (CBRS, CIK 2021728): add to the public financial roster.** It completed its IPO on May 15. Its [August 12 Q2 10-Q](https://investors.cerebras.ai/static-files/cb9030b1-2019-4c97-a10e-411077bd3bd0) reports $180.110 million Q2 GAAP revenue, including $125.991 million *cloud and other services*, versus $33.027 million in that category a year earlier. This is recognized revenue from a shipped-hardware and operating-cloud business, although the category is broader than inference alone. H1 operating cash flow was negative $47.488 million and cash purchases of property and equipment were $548.873 million: cash after those purchases was negative $596.361 million. At June 30 it held $6.742 billion unrestricted cash, $684.680 million restricted cash, and $1.179 billion investments, following $6.233 billion net IPO cash proceeds. It owed $918.244 million on a secured **OpenAI Working Capital Loan**, down from $1.005 billion received through $86.327 million of noncash service credits; the undrawn $850 million revolver is capacity, not outstanding debt. OpenAI also held a warrant for up to 33,445,026 shares at a nominal exercise price, with vesting tied to loan funding and capacity or commercial milestones. The company recorded $822.9 million in OpenAI customer-warrant assets in H1, to be amortized against future revenue. These are a customer, lender, and equity relationship in one contract; they do not make all Cerebras revenue circular. The [August 12 release](https://investors.cerebras.ai/news-releases/news-release-details/cerebras-systems-fast-inference-cloud-business-nearly-quadruples) states $25.4 billion remaining performance obligations and **more than 600 MW comprising both live capacity and capacity contracted for future delivery by end-2027**. Neither figure is 600 MW currently earning revenue. The 10-Q gives no standalone inference-only revenue or deployed-MW utilization.
- **Broadcom (AVGO), Apollo (APO), Blackstone (BX), Athene: one initial transaction.** Apollo's [June 9 capital-solution announcement](https://ir.apollo.com/_assets/_ae80155f051092e0e4d6e9cf9038202d/apollo/news/2026-06-09_Apollo_Leads_35_Billion_Capital_Solution_for_629.pdf) identifies **one $35 billion initial financing tranche** led by Apollo-managed funds and affiliates with Blackstone and banks, drawn across multiple years, to support more than 1 GW of Anthropic compute at Fluidstack-based sites. Broadcom supplies custom XPUs and networking. Broadcom's [September 10 10-Q, Note 10 and p. 25](https://investors.broadcom.com/static-files/96641754-401f-4090-a4ab-210728c83a28) says its five-year lease **backstop** on that arrangement has approximately **$29 billion maximum potential liability after all racks deploy**, with exposure rising on delivery and falling as the tenant pays. A default-loss calculation starts from 85% of covered unpaid leases and subtracts rack resale value. Its reported backstop fair value was immaterial and it had paid nothing under it. Under the same leases, the customer may, if conditions require, issue Broadcom up to **$42 billion convertible promissory notes**, usable only for lease obligations; **none were issued as of August 2**. These are contingent support arrangements **inside the same $35 billion program**, not $71 billion of additional financing. Broadcom's [September 2 fiscal-Q3 release](https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-inc-announces-third-quarter-fiscal-year-2026-financial) separately reports $16.7 billion recognized AI semiconductor revenue; it cannot be assigned entirely to Anthropic or this platform. Apollo's [August 10 company-hosted 10-Q, Purchase Obligation Guarantee note](https://ir.apollo.com/sec-filings/content/0001858681-26-000040/0001858681-26-000040.pdf) explicitly includes **Athene** among the Apollo affiliates. It says Athene Holding Ltd. guarantees **15% of WarehouseCo's outstanding purchase obligation** if that Apollo-managed fund defaults, with a third party reimbursing 35% of any Athene guarantee payment. Apollo does not disclose the dollar amount currently funded by Athene or the outstanding purchase-obligation balance in that note. Athene, Apollo funds, and Apollo consolidated results are exposures to one deal, not three $35 billion financings. The platform's greater-than-20-GW target through 2028 is prospective.
- **NVIDIA, Apollo, BlackRock (BLK), Blackstone, Brookfield (BN/BAM), Goldman Sachs (GS), and KKR: proposed financing network.** The [August 10 joint announcement](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/nvidia-partners-with-apollo-blackrock-blackstone-brookfield-goldman-sachs-and-kkr-to-establish-ai-compute-infrastructure-financing-platforms-to-mobilize-over-500-billion-of-third-party-capital/) says memoranda of understanding seek to mobilize **more than $500 billion of third-party capital over time** for NVIDIA compute. Final agreements remained subject to execution as of the announcement. This is a target across six potential financing partners, not funded capacity or an amount to add to each partner's separate deals. NVIDIA's [August 10-Q and supplier review](semiconductor_supply_2026-09-16.md) separately identify its direct cloud commitments and guarantees.
- **Blue Owl Capital (OWL): funded-equipment exposure to an existing issuer.** [August 28 company announcement](https://www.blueowl.com/news/blue-owl-managed-funds-lead-24-billion-ai-factory-financing-iren) says managed funds led a $2.4 billion IREN equipment financing: $1.2 billion senior term loan plus $1.2 billion secured notes, to be drawn against GPU delivery and commissioning. This is IREN financing and Blue Owl fund exposure to the **same hardware**, not $4.8 billion of independent investment. IREN already belongs to the original issuer cohort; Blue Owl is the capital-provider counterparty. Fund-level retained share and fair-value marks were not disclosed in that announcement.
- **Helix Digital Infrastructure / KKR (KKR), Kuwait Investment Authority, NVIDIA, Vistra (VST): committed platform, not delivered power.** [KKR's June 11 launch](https://media.kkr.com/news-details?news_id=6a26acd6-83b8-4377-84be-b1dadb847806) reports more than $10 billion of long-duration capital commitments to Helix; the four named groups are founding investors, with Vistra preferred for power. No Helix energized MW, recognized customer revenue, or amount drawn was supplied in that release. KKR's [Q2 transcript](https://ir.kkr.com/media/document/f00cf12c-3dfa-4eda-80fc-c25f5552e5b2/assets/KKR_Q226_Earnings_Transcript.pdf?disposition=inline) identifies Helix as its vehicle, so do not count its committed capital again as KKR corporate capex.
- **Blackstone/Google TPU cloud joint venture: a separate, prospective vehicle.** Blackstone's [May 18 announcement](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/blackstone-announces-joint-venture-with-google-to-create-new-tpu-cloud/) commits an initial $5 billion of Blackstone-managed-fund equity and targets the first **500 MW online in 2027**. Google will supply TPUs, software, and services. It is distinct from the Broadcom/Anthropic AI XPV tranche and the August NVIDIA memorandum, but the public release does not specify overlap in future sites or investors. There was no disclosed operating revenue at announcement.

## Cloud operators and data-center developers outside the 22-CIK panel

- **Fireworks AI: operating inference seller with a company-stated run rate.** Its [July 15 Series D announcement](https://fireworks.ai/blog/series-d-announcement) reports $1.505 billion new funding, more than **$1 billion annualized revenue run rate**, and more than 40 trillion tokens served daily. The run rate is an exit-rate claim, not audited trailing-year GAAP revenue. No public cash-flow, capex, debt, or gross-versus-net revenue reconciliation was found in that announcement. This is materially more commercial evidence than a prerevenue research lab.
- **Together AI: operating GPU and inference platform.** Its [July 8 release](https://www.together.ai/blog/provisioned-throughput) says APIs handle more than **400 trillion tokens per month** and introduces committed inference throughput sold by tokens; its [September 10 release](https://www.together.ai/blog/introducing-preemptible-compute-the-same-compute-half-the-price) introduces preemptible GPU clusters. Those are company-reported throughput and offered products, not dollars of realized revenue or margin. Its public statements checked here supply no audited cash-flow or utilization figure.
- **Groq: operating inference cloud and silicon licensor.** Its [June 22 financing release](https://groq.com/newsroom/groq-raises-usd650m-to-scale-its-ai-inference-cloud-business) reports $650 million growth capital, **13 operating data centers**, and more than five million developers, with a target of 200 MW by 2027. The same release says NVIDIA licensed its inference technology on a non-exclusive basis in December 2025. Site count is not aggregate live MW or contracted revenue; company-level financials remain undisclosed in this source.
- **Lambda: operating cloud with rated, asset-backed financing.** Its [August 27 announcement](https://lambda.ai/blog/lambda-closes-926-million-senior-secured-term-loan-b-facility) reports a **closed $926 million** GPU-backed term loan to fund a committed deployment with an unnamed investment-grade customer, priced at SOFR plus 3%, amortizing to December 2030, and rated Baa2 by Moody's. It cites a separate $1 billion senior secured credit facility announced in May. Closed debt and a committed customer show financing and offtake; the number of GPUs deployed, utilization, issuer-wide leverage, and recognized revenue are not given.
- **Nscale: cloud/operator financing and a multi-site development book.** Its [August 31 release](https://www.nscale.com/press-releases/nscale-3-billion-delayed-draw-term-loans) reports about **$3 billion in closed delayed-draw loan commitments** for up to 200 MW of IT load at a planned Texas site and up to 40 MW at a North Carolina retrofit. These are financing commitments and supported site limits, not 240 MW of installed chips. Nscale's [March 9 Series C release](https://www.nscale.com/press-releases/nscale-series-c) reports $2 billion equity raised; it is a separate financing source, not operating revenue. Its [newsroom](https://www.nscale.com/newsroom) lists the July 30 Anyscale acquisition, so Anyscale is part of Nscale's group for entity counting.
- **Crusoe: one live flagship phase amid a much larger contracted/development pipeline.** Its [September 2025 Abilene release](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-announces-flagship-abilene-data-center-is-live) confirms the first two buildings were energized and Oracle delivered GB200 racks. Crusoe's [June 9 2026 release](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoes-contracted-ai-infrastructure-capacity-approaches-5-gigawatts-across-data-centers-and-cloud) states **4.9 GW contracted across data-center projects and its cloud**, and more than 40 GW in a broader development/negotiation pipeline. These are mixed infrastructure stages, not live AI compute. Its [March 27 Microsoft release](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-announces-new-900-mw-ai-factory-campus-in-abilene-texas-to-support-microsoft-ai-infrastructure) targets the first building of a separate 900-MW campus for mid-2027; its existing OpenAI/Oracle Abilene first phase is already part of the 4.9-GW total. The [September 15 Perplexity agreement](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-perplexity-partnership) spans training and managed inference, while Crusoe also buys Perplexity software for staff. The reciprocal purchase deserves transaction-level review before inferring net external demand. Private consolidated revenue, debt and cash capex were not disclosed in these releases.
- **Fluidstack: Anthropic facility and cloud counterparty.** Its [November 2025 announcement](https://fluidstack.io/news/fluidstack-selected-by-anthropic-to-deliver-custom-data-centers-in-the-us) describes a **$50 billion multiyear infrastructure deal** and New York/Texas sites scheduled to come online across 2026. The Broadcom/Apollo/Blackstone initial $35 billion tranche is explicitly intended to finance Anthropic capacity at Fluidstack-based sites; those amounts and sites may overlap and should not be summed as separate projects. No Fluidstack GAAP revenue or site-by-site energized MW was established by the announcement.
- **Vultr: commercial GPU cloud with hardware access, limited financial visibility.** Its [April 7 validation note](https://blogs.vultr.com/nvidia-exemplar-cloud-ai-training) documents a 512-B200-GPU test cluster. Its [July 28 AMD note](https://blogs.vultr.com/amd-advancing-ai-san-francisco-2026) announces a planned Helios/MI455X deployment. The benchmark is a running cluster; the AMD deployment was a collaboration announcement. Neither release supplies company-level revenue, cash flow or contracted MW.
- **Vantage Data Centers: financed development, including sites shared with existing cloud issuers.** Its [September 14 release](https://vantage-dc.com/news/vantage-secures-2b-credit-facility-expanding-financing-to-support-growing-demand/) reports a **closed $2 billion development revolver** and says it has closed more than $40 billion of capital during 2026; that broader number contains multiple funding transactions and is not current capex. Its [August 13 Vantage-Nebius release](https://vantage-dc.com/company/news-events/) identifies a UK deployment with Nebius, already in the issuer cohort. The landlord's MW and Nebius's cloud MW refer to related capacity, not independent demand.
- **Aligned Data Centers / AIP / MGX / BlackRock GIP: closed ownership transaction with mixed capacity.** [July 21 release](https://aligneddc.com/press-release/aip-mgx-and-blackrocks-gip-close-acquisition-of-aligned-data-centers/) says the consortium closed its Aligned acquisition at about **$40 billion enterprise value**; the target's 51 campuses total more than **6.4 GW of operational *and planned* capacity**. Enterprise value is a valuation, not new construction spend. [September 10 announcement](https://aligneddc.com/press-release/project-phoenix-data-center-shippingport-pa/) records groundbreaking for a separate 2-GW Project Phoenix; groundbreaking is not energized supply. Aligned is the operating company, AIP/MGX/GIP its owners, and BlackRock/GIP should not be added again as a second developer.
- **QTS and AirTrunk: Blackstone portfolio developers.** Blackstone's [August 14 portfolio update](https://www.blackstone.com/pattern-recognition/) projects **7 GW of 2026 leasing** across its global data-center platforms, including QTS and AirTrunk, of which the full year had not yet been signed by June 30. This is a landlord lease-flow projection across portfolio companies, not 7 GW energized or a separate 7 GW for each company. Blackstone's projected $100 billion-plus related facility capex and $200 billion-plus customer chip purchases occur at different layers of the same prospective sites. These companies' stand-alone private financials were not supplied in that portfolio update.
- **Digital Realty (DLR): public landlord with booked rents and clear ownership share.** Its [July 23 Q2 release](https://investor.digitalrealty.com/news-releases/news-release-details/digital-realty-reports-second-quarter-2026-results) reports signed Q2 bookings of $307 million annualized GAAP base rent at **100% venture share**, but only $208 million at Digital Realty's share. Two July hyperscale leases add $410 million at 100% and $205 million at its share. It reported $18.6 billion total debt and 4.7x net debt/adjusted EBITDA. These are contracted future rent metrics, not AI-only recognized Q2 revenue. Do not sum 100%-share and attributable-share versions.
- **Equinix (EQIX): public operating landlord.** Its [July 29 Q2 release](https://newsroom.equinix.com/2026-07-29-Equinix-Reports-Second-Quarter-Results%2C-Raises-2026-Guidance-and-Long-Term-Outlook) reports **$2.625 billion recognized quarterly revenue**, up 16% reported, with a one-time xScale fee contributing. It had 52 expansion projects underway and $424 million annualized gross bookings. These company-wide totals include traditional colocation and interconnection; AI-only revenue and site-level energized MW are not specified here.
- **Lancium: power/land developer in Crusoe's campus chain.** Crusoe's [July 15 Childress announcement](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-and-lancium-announce-1-gigawatt-ai-data-center-campus-in-childress-texas) identifies Lancium as the land/power partner for a **planned 1-GW** campus. Crusoe also builds on Lancium-owned Abilene land. Do not add Lancium's campus MW to Crusoe's as if they were separate powered loads; no Childress energized load is established by the announcement.
- **SB Energy:** Already in the [company register](company_tracking_2026-09-16.md). Its proposed OpenAI PORTS-Pike lease and NVIDIA contingent guarantee describe a future, phased site; SB Energy disclosed no data-center revenue by its September 1 prospectus. Do not duplicate its 4.25-GW project under OpenAI, NVIDIA and SB Energy.

## Counting rules and what remains unknown

Track the legal entity, physical campus, computing equipment, financier and paying customer as separate linked records. The same Anthropic rack can appear in Fluidstack's site announcement, Broadcom's XPU sales, Apollo/Blackstone's financing, and Anthropic's compute expense. The $35 billion financing, $29 billion maximum contingent Broadcom backstop, conditional $42 billion convertible-note facility, Athene guarantee, and future 1-GW deployment are **different attributes of one program**. Crusoe/Lancium/OpenAI/Oracle describe related Abilene assets. Aligned/AIP/MGX/GIP describe an operating company and its owners. Bit Digital/WhiteFiber share consolidated capex; Cursor/SpaceX share a parent after August 14. These links need explicit entity and project IDs before any aggregate ecosystem dollars or GW are calculated.

**Reported Anthropic offtake to two more tracked clouds:** [Reuters reported](https://ca.marketscreener.com/news/anthropic-to-rent-ai-computing-power-from-nscale-for-45-billion-source-says-ce7858d9de8af121) on August 26 that Anthropic agreed to roughly $45 billion over six years for about 460 MW at Nscale's planned West Virginia campus; Anthropic declined comment in that report. [Reuters reported](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/anthropic-signs-35-billion-cloud-005022618.html) on August 31 a roughly $35 billion Anthropic/Lambda compute agreement. The latter syndicated article surfaced in search but returned HTTP 429 on direct open, so its detailed terms are not used here. [Nscale's own release](https://www.nscale.com/press-releases/nscale-acquires-american-intelligence-power-corporation) confirms its Monarch campus acquisition and power runway, but not Anthropic's identity or the reported contract amount. [Hut 8's July release](https://www.hut8.com/news-insights/press-releases/hut-8-fully-commercializes-1-gw-beacon-point-ai-data-center-campus-with-second-352-mw-it-lease) confirms two 352 MW Beacon Point IT leases without naming the end user. Keep those landlord leases, cloud offtake, and equipment financing linked by source and site rather than adding their face values as independent customer demand. The $45 billion and $35 billion remain reporting-tier leads pending primary contract disclosure.

The strongest newly found *recognized* demand evidence here is Cerebras's GAAP cloud-services growth and Broadcom's realized AI-chip sales. Commercial operators disclose substantial token throughput; Fireworks's $1 billion run rate is a company estimate at one point in time. The strongest newly found financing-dependence evidence is Cerebras's OpenAI loan and customer warrants, Broadcom's lease backstop and conditional notes, Athene's purchase-obligation guarantee, and the increasing use of GPU-backed loans at Lambda and Nscale. Both observations can hold at once. A sector-wide cash return, 2027 realized utilization, and global installed AI-server GW remain unmeasured by this company roster.

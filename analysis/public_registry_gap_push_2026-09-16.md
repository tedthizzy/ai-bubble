# Additional public-record systems acquired on September 16, 2026 Pacific

Three official source systems extend the September refresh beyond SEC filings: Connecticut's statewide UCC and business registry exports, Hillsborough County's monthly civil case files, and the county clerk's daily official-record index. The raw exports contain personal names and addresses and remain under gitignored `data/off_edgar_expansion_2026-09-16/`. Only aggregate counts, source URIs, file hashes, and validation results appear here. These sources have not yet been entity-joined into the dated whole-economy graph.

Connecticut's [Secretary of the State UCC export](https://data.ct.gov/d/xfev-8smz) contained 846,268 rows and 436,971 distinct original filing numbers. The source refreshed September 16 at 12:33 a.m. Pacific. The complete CSV had 212,635,169 bytes and SHA-256 `f5cbf939d3380c77d479296d2d17dd831929b136ed342f121e772740ea9f01ce`; the parsed row count exactly matched the state's API count before and after download. There were 569,147 rows with a business debtor name and 518,941 rows marked `ORIG FIN STMT`; these are row counts, not separate borrowers or outstanding loans. The state describes coverage as active liens plus liens less than one year past natural lapse, and includes non-UCC vessel, aircraft, tax, and municipal liens. The exposed schema has no loan principal or current balance, so it cannot apply this repo's $1 million materiality floor to debt.

The [Connecticut Business Master](https://data.ct.gov/d/n7gp-d28j) contained 1,297,619 unique business IDs, including 460,636 with source status `Active`. Its September 16 source refresh was at 1:54 a.m. Pacific. The full CSV had 543,062,823 bytes and SHA-256 `1d295403ec265adaa5b345439add57660d911de0cf55bdc084705095400df9af`. The [Business Filing History](https://data.ct.gov/d/ah3s-bes7) contained 10,067,114 rows, including 438,919 whose filing-date string begins with 2026 and an observed latest filing date of September 15. Its source refresh was September 16 at 1:58 a.m. Pacific; the CSV had 1,652,204,916 bytes and SHA-256 `b8bf72b15ce6b6dbbe366e05896e4c35f4cb07d1098208b9a5e058cda8bbb577`. Both exports matched state API counts before and after acquisition. History references 1,275,475 distinct IDs found in Business Master, 316 distinct IDs absent from that snapshot, and eight rows with an empty business ID. Registry dissolution, forfeiture, or revocation is an administrative filing/status, not proof of insolvency or AI-related distress. The master/history join requires an explicit unmatched-ID bucket.

The [Hillsborough County Clerk](https://www.hillsclerk.com/ca/records-and-reports/public-data-files) says its [civil bulk files](https://publicrec.hillsclerk.com/Civil/bulkdata/) cover Circuit and County Civil cases by monthly filing period and remain available for one year. All 12 available monthly case files and 12 event files were acquired. The case files contain 77,462 rows and 77,221 distinct case numbers, with filing dates from September 1, 2025 through August 31, 2026. The associated event files contain 835,930 rows. The latest file was posted September 10 and covers August filings. A case row can repeat, and events are records linked to the reported cases; neither count is a national court-caseload or financial-distress numerator. The retained batch manifest lists each original file URL, byte count, row count, and SHA-256.

The Clerk's [Official Records daily index](https://publicrec.hillsclerk.com/OfficialRecords/DailyIndexes/) exposed 38 complete D/P/M file triplets, 114 files in all, for export dates July 14 through September 3, 2026. Its [format guide](https://publicrec.hillsclerk.com/OfficialRecords/DailyIndexes/readme.txt) defines D as recorded or modified documents, P as related parties, and M as a document-code map. The complete acquisition contained 78,248 D document rows, 213,050 P party rows, and 2,812 M map rows. Every file's parsed record count equaled its `EOF` control count. The guide's five-field P layout is stale: the current files carry a sixth instrument-number field, which the parser checks. The D rows contain 77,766 distinct instrument numbers, so adding daily D rows would count at least some instruments twice. Of the D rows, 10,019 have a populated consideration field and 396 show at least $1 million; 383 of those 396 carry deed code `D`, ten carry mortgage code `MTG`, and three have other codes. The guide calls consideration a sales-price field. This is a county recording value, not a current loan balance or AI/data-center investment. No parcel or entity match to a data-center project was established. The newest available export date, September 3, was 13 days before this audit's September 16 cutoff.

The local manifests are `data/off_edgar_expansion_2026-09-16/connecticut/acquisition_manifest.json`, `.../hillsborough_civil/acquisition_manifest.json`, and `.../hillsborough_official_records/acquisition_manifest.json`. Their SHA-256 hashes are, respectively, `7eed5572cf892af85788d321e7858b159ae457c3ddc3aa29d3ae492b2c248c72`, `2dab10abf73d8228d2b886dc83343b06448a8317edf1c1689adf7ed2b37895ea`, and `b932c9c33504e786322926c120480e44fabd71a9dac94bb441d91aa620db6acd`. `scripts/audit_public_registry_acquisitions_2026_09_16.py` rehashed all 141 files and emitted 141 file-level provenance rows with source URI, content hash, retrieval time, source grain, and parsed row count. The existing source-invariant audit passed on that ledger with 141 URI values checked, zero violations, and zero warnings. The ledger represents 13,418,503 source rows across different record grains; that sum is not a unique-entity count.

These are additive source acquisitions, not a completed US registry, court, or deeds census. Connecticut UCC omits many lapsed historical liens and has no exposure amounts. The Florida files cover one county and have a publication lag. Other states, other counties, federal PACER dockets, company-level insurer holdings, and current patent bulk access remain outside this acquisition. No national prevalence or AI bubble probability is computed from the new registries.

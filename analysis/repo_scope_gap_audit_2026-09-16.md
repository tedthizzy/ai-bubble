# September 16 repo-scope gap audit

This is a read-only audit of what the current refresh can support. It distinguishes a fresh case-zero research result from a refreshed whole-economy verdict. The SEC acquisition and joined capital graph were still running when this audit was written. Counts below describe opened local files, not an assertion about every public record that exists.

1. **The economy-wide verdict is still June.** `analysis/ATLAS_INDEX.md:7-32` retains the 450 deep profiles, 174 confirmed names, one direct contagion link, and an approximately 62% worsening trajectory. `analysis/ATLAS_INDEX.md:3` says its economy-wide census remains dated June. The September XBRL screen covers 8,022 exchange-ticker CIKs, of which 7,021 have nonempty companyfacts; 4,415 lack enough matched facts for a reliable classification (`analysis/economy_xbrl_fragility_2026-09-16.md:1-3`). The June entity census contains 789,787 names and only 2,356 matched expanded CIKs (`data/entity_universe/entity_universe.summary.json`); it has not been rebuilt from the September source acquisition. A fresh screen is not a fresh adjudication of the June 174 or the broader private/SPV universe. **Fix:** after the SEC and non-SEC inputs finish, construct a dated input tree, run `scripts/build_entity_universe.py --data-dir <dated-input-root> --output-dir <new-dated-output> --sec-reference-json <saved-reference> --no-fetch-sec-reference`, and make `scripts/economy_wide_signature_scan.py` accept those dated paths and a new output prefix. Its current inputs and outputs are hardwired to June-era `data/capital`, `data/edgar_acquisition`, `data/entity_universe`, and `analysis/economy_wide_fragility_map` (`scripts/economy_wide_signature_scan.py:8-40`). Inspect and verify each new top-ranked issuer before making any economy-wide prevalence or epicenter claim. The universe builder reads only the seven paths declared in `src/bubble/ingestion/entities/universe.py:159-282`, so the dated input tree must map the September FERC, GLEIF, queue, tracker, permit, equipment, and SEC files to those paths. Do not simply point it at the repo's `data` directory and call the result current.

2. **The acquired non-SEC catalog is deep in a few sources, not broad across the declared whole-economy source list.** The September summary reports 483 of 484 acquisition targets fetched, 5,011,884 parsed rows, and 467 FERC PPA page targets (`analysis/source_refresh_2026-09-16.json`). These are valid acquisition counts. They do not mean 483 independent source systems or coverage of all dimensions in `analysis/total_ecosystem_dive.md:68-80`. The current catalog builder's public seeds are principally ISO queues, EIA/EPA, and project trackers (`src/bubble/ingestion/sources/catalog_builder.py:78-240`). The source-refresh record itself names missing local zoning, state PUC, broad utility energization, foreign data, and fresh authenticated satellite observations. The June boundary ledger also leaves UCC, WARN, deeds, courts, private debt marks, insurer statutory data, patents, and transcripts open or access-limited (`analysis/boundary_ledger.md:33-41,51-69`). **Fix:** add dated source coverage by *system and dimension*, with `attempted`, `acquired`, `not checked`, `access blocked`, and `no public disclosure` states. Start with accessible public WARN, utility/PUC energization records, USPTO, and country-specific corporate filings for connected entities. Keep account-gated or billable sources out until their access and cost are authorized. The already written state-utility and policy reviews are useful research, but they are not rows in the reusable entity/capital pipeline.

S1b needs a record of pulled or postponed offerings, and S4 needs a comparable roughly annual basket of company-stated OpenAI, Anthropic, and Microsoft-AI run rates (`analysis/preregistered_signals.md:22-26,62-70`; `scripts/run_dated_engine_2026_09_16.py:81-123`). SEC filing acquisition can surface filed issuer events for CONFIRM-3 but cannot prove that no private SPV attempted and failed to sell debt. **Fix:** reconcile every discoverable priced, postponed, and pulled cluster issue against `analysis/issuance_cards.json`, recording search venue and coverage. Add company-stated basket prints to `analysis/demand_trajectory.json` only if definitions and comparison dates satisfy the registered S4 rule. Preserve `unmeasured` where they do not. The current September bubble-probability field correctly remains null; the June `0.67` and `0.25` are historical (`README.md:3,11-12`).

No deployment state was checked in this audit.

6. **The old atlas's power-status totals can be refreshed locally, with a narrower interpretation.** `analysis/ATLAS_INDEX.md:13` cites April EIA-860M planned 288,406 MW and canceled/postponed 181,264 MW; the source is recorded in `analysis/eia_860m_buildout.json`. The September acquisition already includes the July 2026 EIA-860M generator workbook in `data/source_acquisition_2026-09-16/non_gleif/source_rows/equipment_records.csv`. A read-only sum of the `Nameplate Capacity (MW)` field for source `eia-860m-generator-inventory-july-2026`, excluding the separate Puerto Rico sheets and sheet-header rows, gives Planned 2,342 rows / 291,137.9 MW and Canceled or Postponed 1,735 rows / 184,141.4 MW. These are all-generation inventory statuses, not AI data-center capacity or a flow of cancellations since April. **Fix:** write a dated status summary from the acquired source rows, preserve workbook hash and sheet/status definitions, and link it beside the old atlas figures. Do not use generation cancellations as proof of empty AI compute halls.

The September case-zero conclusion can be reported now with those limits: stronger disclosed model/cloud demand, larger financed and equity-valued buildout, no registered CONFIRM-2 transmission, and no verified 2027 fleetwide utilization or glut.

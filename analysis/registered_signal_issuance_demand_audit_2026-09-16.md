## Registered read

**S1 is contra, at 180 basis points.** The latest completed bond in the carded AI/data-center cluster is the $3.9 billion QTS Central Issuer / QTS TRS Central Issuer secured construction offering that [transaction counsel says closed August 27](https://www.stblaw.com/about-us/news/view/2026/09/01/qts-completes-inaugural-construction-rule-144a-notes-offering). A [fund holder's September 10 portfolio](https://carboncollectivefunds.com/ccsb/) identifies CUSIP 74751QAA6, 6.625% coupon and September 1, 2031 maturity. [FRED DGS5](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DGS5&cosd=2026-08-20&coed=2026-09-15) was 4.83% on September 15. The registered coupon-minus-current-five-year-Treasury proxy is therefore (6.625 - 4.83) x 100 = 179.5, rounded to 180 basis points, below the 600 basis point contra threshold. The August 27 date is **closing**, not a verified pricing trade date. QTS's issue price and issue yield were not found in reviewed public issuer/counsel documents. The holder's 98.90 is a September 10 secondary mark and is not an issue price. A [translated Bloomberg report](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20260824000024) puts placement yield around 7.228%; that unverified value is not used in registered S1. Even that reported yield would be about 240 basis points above this Treasury observation, below the 600 basis point cut. An investment-grade Microsoft-linked secured QTS project is not credit-equivalent to the earlier CoreWeave corporate or Galaxy project bonds. Books are not issuer-confirmed, so this single print does not meet TIMING-KILL's through-Q4 requirement.

**S1b is confirming on original reporting.** [Bloomberg Law on July 21](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/distress-watch-data-center-debt-hits-a-wall-after-deal-rush) reported that Prime Data Centers paused a planned bond and Pure Data Centres abandoned a proposed EUR 1 billion bond in favor of bank financing. [Bloomberg's July 22 follow-up](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/artificial-intelligence/ai-borrowers-are-pushing-a-niche-credit-market-to-its-limits-1), reproduced in fuller form by [BusinessMirror](https://businessmirror.com.ph/2026/07/22/ai-borrowers-pushing-niche-credit-market-to-limits/), says Prime reduced its Norwegian-law proposal from $600 million to $500 million before shelving it after investor resistance. The exact decision dates remain unverified, so both cards use the July 21 observation date. These are original journalistic observations, not issuer admissions. [Pure's July 21 issuer announcement](https://puredc.com/2026/07/21/pure-dc-secures-additional-e1-3-billion-to-accelerate-growth-of-one-of-europes-largest-ai-campuses/) confirms EUR 1.3 billion of committed bank financing for phase one of its 110 MW Finland AI campus. It confirms continuing project access to **another** financing venue, not the bond withdrawal itself. Registered S1b says any carded postponed or pulled cluster print confirms; it does not require issuer default or a systemwide closed credit window.

S2 is confirming at a +2.04 percentage point CCC-minus-HY year-to-date widening, while S3 is contra at +1.5 percentage points of exposed-versus-control BDC discount. CONFIRM-2 is false. S1b satisfies the bond-event leg of CONFIRM-1; the separate pending-maturity-or-DDTL-draw condition must be tied to the refreshed issuer schedule before a final compound call. No CONFIRM-3 issuer event follows from these bond transactions.

**S4 remains unmeasured for a September comparison.** The fixed basket requires OpenAI total annualized revenue, Anthropic run-rate revenue and Microsoft AI business ARR, with company-stated prints and a roughly annual comparable prior basket. [OpenAI's March 31 statement](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/) says $2 billion of revenue **per month**, which annualizes to $24 billion at that point. [Anthropic's May 28 statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h) says run-rate revenue **crossed** $47 billion earlier in May. [Microsoft's April 29 FY26 Q3 release](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/Investor/earnings/FY-2026-Q3/press-release-webcast) says AI business annual revenue run rate **surpassed** $37 billion, up 123% year over year. Their $108 billion sum is an approximate, asynchronous floor, not a September-quarter actual revenue total. [OpenAI's August 31 ads announcement](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/) gives $1 billion annualized for **ChatGPT Ads**, a component of OpenAI revenue; substituting it for the whole company or adding it to the March total would change the registered definition or double count. The [FY26 Q4 Microsoft investor event](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4) was reviewed without finding a replacement AI business ARR amount. The registered June +253% growth read is a historical approximation from a weak retro-carded July 2025 Anthropic baseline, an implied Microsoft baseline and non-synchronized print dates. It must not be represented as a newly measured September YoY value.

Other relevant priced paper was inspected without forcing it into the five-year AI-compute S1 comparison. [Hut 8's Beacon Point DC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1964789/000110465926070744/tm2616926d1_ex99-1.htm) priced $4.25 billion of fully amortizing 6.129% secured notes due **2042** on June 4, a very different duration. [CreditFlow's August 13 new-issue tape](https://creditflowresearch.com/post-128892) records Zenith Arc's $2.25 billion 8.875% secured notes at 99.500 for a Jane Street-leased Oklahoma data center, and [Janus Henderson holdings](https://www.janushenderson.com/en-us/advisor/product/income-etf-jiii/full-holdings/) independently identify its coupon and CUSIP. The [project site](https://www.okmulgeefluidstack.io/) names Jane Street as first tenant but does not disclose that the contracted capacity serves AI workloads. Zenith is therefore a data-center credit comparator, not asserted as a registered AI-compute S1 issuer. Bloomberg's September 15 description of the bond as issued in "early September" conflicts with CreditFlow's precise August 13 pricing and August 18 settlement; this audit uses CreditFlow's transaction tape and flags the discrepancy. Long-dated data-center project notes, CMBS, floating-rate loans, GPU equipment loans and convertibles need separate rate/tenor analysis.

The open September calendar is material but is not an S1b failure until an attempted sale is documented as postponed, pulled or failed. [Octus's September 9 original reporting](https://octus.com/resources/articles/primary-market-braces-for-multibillion-dollar-data-center-wave-in-post-labor-day-rush/) describes premarketing of an Applied Digital $3.5 billion Delta Forge 1 bond, a roughly $1.15 billion CoreWeave-tenanted Digital Drive secured bond and an $800 million Novva bond. It explicitly calls the quoted 7%-9% ranges unofficial price whispers. A searched public issuer/pricing record did not establish completed pricing or a withdrawal for these three by this cutoff; their status remains **pending/unknown**, not `failed`. The same Octus report also describes Volta's planned $5 billion loan and Nexus's roughly $15 billion bridge financing. These are distinct from completed bond prints.

Outside straight project bonds, [Nebius's August convertible pricing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926098924/tm2623617d1_ex99-1.htm) and [closing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926100347/tm2623863d1_6k.htm), [WhiteFiber's August convertible closing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2042022/000121390026092599/ea0302925-8k_white.htm), [Blue Owl's IREN equipment financing](https://www.blueowl.com/news/blue-owl-managed-funds-lead-24-billion-ai-factory-financing-iren) and [CoreWeave's $2.6 billion August loan](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000357/ex991pr.htm) show continued access to equity-linked, secured and bank/private-credit channels. Convertible coupons embed equity optionality; equipment loans and project loans have different collateral and maturity. None supplies a directly comparable fixed-rate S1 coupon.

The search covered the carded issuers' linked filings/releases, transaction counsel, disclosed holders, FRED, original Bloomberg/Octus/CreditFlow reports and public issuer searches through September 16. Private 144A issue documents, exact QTS issue yield/price, full order books, closed-loan draw schedules and an exhaustive global aborted-deal register were not available in these reviewed public sources. That limit prevents a universal absence claim and limits any interpretation beyond the registered selected-card signals.

## Later September 16 correction: CONFIRM-1 pending schedule

The [CoreWeave 10-Q filed August 12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000366/crwv-20260630.htm), Note 10, reports $4.413 billion of debt principal payments for the remaining portion of calendar 2026 and $6.184 billion for 2027, measured at June 30. It reports $10.0 billion of undrawn availability at June 30 across its revolver and DDTL 3.0, 4.0 and 5.0 facilities. The DDTL 4.0 contract provides $8.5 billion of capacity with $2.9 billion drawn at June 30 and a draw window through June 30, 2027. DDTL 5.0 provides $3.1 billion of capacity with $1.101 billion drawn at June 30 and a draw window through September 30, 2026. These are capacity and reporting-date balances, not proof of what remained undrawn on September 16. Facility draws also have borrowing conditions and asset-based limits.

The [August 7 DDTL 5.5 agreement filed in an 8-K on August 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000357/crwv-20260807.htm) adds $2.6 billion of delayed-draw capacity through December 2026 and matures September 1, 2031. The August 12 10-Q's subsequent-events note says $1.2 billion had been drawn after closing. This later facility illustrates continuing draw scheduling but did not exist when Bloomberg reported the July 21 Prime and Pure bond decisions. The selected CoreWeave submissions manifest has no later CoreWeave 10-Q, 10-K or 8-K through September 16; later rows are ownership forms. It does not establish an undisclosed draw balance or that all June 30 principal payments remained due.

The July 21 reported Prime and Pure bond events meet the registered S1b event leg while CoreWeave's DDTL 4.0 and 5.0 draw windows and disclosed principal schedule were pending in the cluster. **CONFIRM-1 is satisfied at a mixed press/filing evidence tier** under the registered cluster-wide wording. This does not establish that CoreWeave's financing caused either bond decision, that Prime or Pure had the same debt schedule, or that credit was closed to the cluster. Pure's separate EUR 1.3 billion bank financing continued. CONFIRM-2 remains false and this compound call does not generate a September bubble probability.

## Limits

- Source review covers the selected issuer corpus, not every site or private contract.
- Missing figures remain null. Annual contract averages are not realized cash flows.
- No parent debt allocation, GPU economic life or ultimate creditor position is inferred.
- Original contractual payment schedules are not confirmations of payments, balances, completion dates or absence of later modifications.
- Cash-margin and revenue-factor sensitivities are assumptions, not forecasts or probabilities.
- Revenue factors model nonpayment or rollover stress; take-or-pay rent does not mechanically vary with GPU utilization.
- Facility principal and issuer carrying balances overlap and must not be summed.
- Current DSCR is absent when the cash-flow period, debt perimeter or full payment schedule is missing.

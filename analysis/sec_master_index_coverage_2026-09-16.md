# SEC master-index coverage, September 16, 2026

The official SEC quarterly master indexes provide an independent filing denominator for June 1 through September 15. The Q3 file's latest filing date is September 15. The September 16 daily `master.20260916.idx` and `.idx.gz` requests both returned HTTP 403 at the check time, so the independent index does not cover September 16. The selected-CIK submission manifest does contain 1,654 September 16 rows. The index and manifest have different cutoffs and must not be presented as one complete September 16 census.

Across the saved Q2 and Q3 indexes, 367,371 distinct complete-submission filing URLs belong to 75,613 CIKs in the June 1-September 15 window. The repo's selection of 8,060 CIKs covers 7,158 of those index filers. The other 68,455 index CIKs filed 205,983 submissions. The selected CIKs account for 161,388 indexed filings, and **all 161,388 are present** in the new 163,063-row submissions manifest when joined by `(CIK, accession)`. There are zero form disagreements. The 1,675 manifest rows absent from the saved indexes comprise 1,654 September 16 rows and 21 earlier rows: ten EFFECT notices, six CORRESP, four UPLOAD, and one September 15 Form 425. This establishes selected-CIK filing-list completeness through September 15 against the saved index. It does not establish completeness of all SEC filers or downloaded document text.

**Denominator correction, later September 16:** The official files contain 367,387 raw index rows, 367,371 unique `(CIK, accession)` pairs, and 257,675 unique accessions. One accession can appear under multiple cofiling CIKs, so a Feed archive should match by accession and then expand to every indexed CIK. Sixteen rows repeat a `(CIK, accession)` with a different form label: CIK 1326200 is indexed under both SC 13D/A and SC TO-T/A. The by-form counts above attribute each pair to its first index label; both labels remain in the raw-form counts in `data/sec_master_index_2026-09-16/coverage_2026-06-01_to_2026-09-16_v2.json`. They do not imply 367,371 distinct underlying document bundles. All earlier CIK and selected-manifest coverage counts refer to unique `(CIK, accession)` pairs.

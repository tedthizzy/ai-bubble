## The reframe (this is the headline)
**The largest AI-DC megaprojects are deliberately built BEHIND-THE-METER (off-grid, on-site gas generation) to
bypass the multi-year ISO interconnection queues — so they have AIR-PERMIT handles but, BY DESIGN, NO
interconnection-queue ID.** My physical lane's "0 MW firm grid evidence / 98.9% no interconnection record" is
therefore not just *unassessable* — for the flagship projects it is *structural*: there is no grid queue to find
because they intentionally don't connect. **The correct evidence handle for these is the PERMIT docket, not an ISO
queue number.** (Verified, 3-0; the off-grid intent is well-corroborated by the WV HB 2014 "sidestep PJM" framing.)

1. **Evidence-tier fix:** for AI-DC megaprojects, search PERMIT dockets (TCEQ / state DEP / county health) and PUC
   approvals, NOT ISO generation queues. Re-tier the physical module: PERMIT_EVIDENCED > ANNOUNCEMENT_ONLY; stop
   treating "no ISO queue" as "no evidence" for off-grid projects.

   gas; Stargate/xAI hundreds of MW) — carrying **emissions/permit-litigation risk** (TransGas + xAI both in
   litigation) AND **stranded-asset risk** (gas plants built for one DC) that the grid-centric physical module misses
   entirely. The off-grid gas buildout is itself a hidden-risk dimension.
